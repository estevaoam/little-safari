# Surface: GitHub Pages site (docs/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Arc users who miss Little Arc. Action: download the latest release (GitHub Releases),
secondary: view on GitHub. Install steps live on the page as a friendly, honest section. Avoid: fake Apple page,
too jokey, dev-tool look, generic SaaS template. Demo is built in HTML/CSS (no real screenshots exist; never fake them).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is one pop-up book, and the little Safari window is paper engineering that stands up off the page,
the same spring the app performs. It refuses the centered headline over a device shot and the row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched coral cover board (#e7563a family) as ground; bright white card-stock pages with soft fold
shading and real cast shadows; ink near-black; one Safari-blue for live links. Display in SF Rounded (ui-rounded,
self-hosted rounded fallback), body in SF. Paper mechanics as controls: pull-tabs, lift-flaps, V-folds, hinges.

STORY: An Arc refugee recognises "Little Arc, for Safari.", watches a link get pulled and a window pop up on the same
page, learns it's real Safari that stays on their Space, downloads, follows the install spread, reads the fine print.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline in white across the coral top, large. Below, an open book spread fills the width: left page
holds a plain-language lede (links from other apps open in a little Safari window right where you are), Download for
Mac (ink) and the version line; right page holds a pasted mini Mac screen with a chat app, a numbered three-step list
under it, and a Play again pill. The little Safari window stands on a crease over the chat app. The hinge sits on the
right page, not the gutter: a gutter hinge would cover the Download button.

FORM: Pop-up book (paper engineering), grounded list position 1 of re-roll round 1, chosen as IMPECCABLE'S PICK.
seed:b4a27dfd3b646082

Signature interaction: the hero demo autoplays once (link clicked, window pops up, pill clicked, window folds away
with a "Sent to your main Safari window" toast), its steps lighting in sync; and the Spaces spread plays the same click
twice side by side: without Little Safari the screen slides to Space 1, with it a little window pops up on Space 3.
Motion grammar: things hinge; the only slide is the Space switch, because it mirrors macOS. (User feedback, 2026-10-24:
the earlier pull-tab and Spaces puzzle didn't explain the product; replaced.)
