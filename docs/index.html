Little Arc, for Safari.

Links you click in Slack, Mail or any other app open in a little Safari window right where you are.

No more getting thrown to whichever desktop your Safari window lives on. Glance at the page, close it, or keep it with one click. It’s real Safari, with your logins, passwords and extensions.

Sent to your main Safari window

  1. Click a link in any app.
  2. A little Safari window pops up, right here.
  3. Worth keeping? Send it to your main Safari window.

Stay on your Space.

Say Safari lives on Space 1 and you’re working in Slack on Space 3. Click a link and macOS throws you over to Space 1, away from what you were doing.

With Little Safari, the page comes to you instead. It opens in a little window on Space 3, and when you’re done, you’re still where you were.

Keep it, or let it go.

The little window is a real Safari window, so Reader, AutoFill, passwords and your other extensions all work. When a page turns out to be worth keeping, send it to your main window. Lift a flap to see how.

Set it up in a few minutes.

This build isn’t signed by Apple (that takes a paid developer account), so macOS and Safari ask you to confirm a couple of things. Tick the steps off as you go.

One quirk: Safari turns “Allow unsigned extensions” off every time it quits. Links still open in little windows, but the Open in Safari button waits until you switch it back on.

The fine print

  • No floating over full-screen apps. macOS doesn’t let one app raise another app’s windows, so the little window opens on a regular desktop.
  • Moving a page reloads it. Safari extensions can’t move tabs between windows, so anything typed into a form stays behind.
  • Changed your mind? Click Restore Safari as Default in the Little Safari window, then delete the app.
